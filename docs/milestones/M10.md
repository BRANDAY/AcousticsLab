## Done (fast pass)

- **`AAmphitheatreActor`** (`Source/AcousticsLab/Public/Levels/AmphitheatreActor.h`, `Private/Levels/AmphitheatreActor.cpp`):
  three `UDynamicMeshComponent`s built with GeometryCore's `FDynamicMesh3` (the mesh type under Geometry Script;
  `Engine/Source/Runtime/GeometryCore/Public/DynamicMesh/DynamicMesh3.h`):
  - *ArenaFloor*: the arena ellipse (semi-axes 32.5 × 20 m), facing up, `AcMat:Stone`;
  - *Cavea*: one closed shell swept around the arena with shared ring vertices: row k has a riser at offset (k−1)·0.8 m
    and a tread at k·0.75 m from offset (k−1)·0.8 to k·0.8 m; the back wall at 16 m offset (top row at 15 m) and the
    ground face close it. "Offset o" = ellipse with semi-axes (32.5 + o, 20 + o) — the same rule as
    `UAmphitheatrePositionsAsset::GetSeatPositionMeters`, so R1–R4 sit 1.1 m above their treads. 96 segments around.
    `AcMat:Stone`, or `AcMat:Audience` (α 0.7) with **audience** on;
  - *Velarium*: a closed 0.1 m ring over the cavea at 18 m (open over the arena), `AcMat:Absorber` (α 0.9); with
    **velarium** off the mesh is empty and the component has no `Acoustic` tag.
  - Triangles are wound per face from the wanted normal (treads up, risers toward the arena, back wall outward), complex
    collision (walkable). Console: `Acoustics.Amphitheatre.Audience 1|0`, `Acoustics.Amphitheatre.Velarium 1|0`, then
    run the solver again.
- **New material preset `Stone`** (α 0.02, s 0.20, `AcousticMaterialPresets.cpp`): limestone, SPEC range 0.02–0.05.
- **Cavea receiver grid**: `AReceiverGrid::CustomTileCentersMeters / CustomTileRows` (explicit tiles instead of the
  regular rectangle), `UHeatmapComponent::BuildTilesAt`. `AAmphitheatreActor::ApplyGrid` fills grid "Cavea": a 2 m plan
  grid (GridSpacingMeters of the data asset) clipped to the cavea, each point on its row's tread (row found by bisection
  on the offset ellipses), receiver 1.1 m above. 856 points.
- **Level_Amphitheatre** (`Tools/Editor/create_m10_content.py`, python commandlet): stand-in floor removed,
  amphitheatre + grid added, the simulation manager runs the ray tracer (20 000 rays, grid included) on BeginPlay,
  PlayerStart above the cavea looking at the arena.
- **Experiments library** `Simulation/UAcousticsExperimentLibrary` (`RunShoeboxAlphaSweep`, `RunAmphitheatreConditions`)
  + `Tools/Experiments/run_m10_experiments.py` (headless); results below and in `docs/results/`.

Reading:
- **Audience** is the dominant change: C80 rises from ≈ 0 dB to ≈ 10.5 dB on every row and T30 becomes ≈ 1.0–1.2 s.
- **Empty stone** (α 0.02): C80 ≈ 0 dB and no measurable T30 within 3 s: energy circulates between the bare vertical
  risers of the closed ellipse (a "whispering gallery" of 20 concentric walls), while the treads send energy up and
  out. This is a property of the idealised geometry (continuous risers, no seats, no openings, s = 0.2) — see Known
  issues.
- **Velarium** alone changes little (the trapped energy travels below the 15 m top); with the audience it shortens
  nothing measurable either (≈ 0.0–0.5 dB). The flat ring at 18 m only catches rays that leave the cavea upwards.
- R4 (side sector, major axis) has a higher C80 than R1–R3 in the empty case (6.6 dB vs −2 dB).

## Known simplifications (SPEC §6 and found here)

- Energy model only (no phase), broadband (NumBands = 1), no diffraction, no frequency-dependent scattering.
- Refracted beam with one apex (least-squares) — approximation (M6).
- Rows are offset ellipses with equal growth of both semi-axes, not true parallel curves of the arena ellipse (same rule
  as the positions asset); the rows are bare steps (no seats, no vomitoria, no podium wall, no outer façade).
- The audience is a material swap of the whole cavea shell (α 0.7) — no extra volume of people.
- The velarium is a flat absorbing ring at 18 m, not a sloped tensile roof.
- Open air: rays leaving over the top are lost (no sky / ground beyond the cavea), so late energy comes from the cavea
  and the arena only.
- Receivers are 0.5 m spheres; the per-cell metrics of the grid are noisy at 20 000 rays (few late hits per sphere at
  30–50 m); the per-row means smooth this.
- Geometry from `RenderData` needs Allow CPU Access (static meshes); the amphitheatre uses dynamic meshes.
- Industry plugins: IR only through the recorded click (M9), Audio Tracing not runnable on this GPU.

## Known issues

- **Empty amphitheatre has no measurable T30** (E5): with stone α 0.02 / s 0.2 and continuous vertical risers the
  energy keeps circulating along the cavea for longer than the 3 s histogram; the Schroeder fit then fails or gives
  > 10 s. Probably too idealised (no seats / openings / scattering of real masonry); to check with a longer TMax and a
  higher scattering of the stone.
- Per-tile metrics of the grid are noisy at the PIE ray count (40 000 rays for the screenshots, 20 000 saved in the level);
  E5 uses 200 000 rays in receiver batches. The ray tracer keeps a histogram per receiver and chunk, so 856 receivers ×
  128 chunks would need ~2.6 GB — the PIE run stays at ≤ 40 000 rays.
- The level description in the HUD still says "Geometry Script build comes in M10" (LevelSetupActor display text).
- The velarium ring hides part of the heatmap in the view from the PlayerStart.
- `bAudience` / `bVelarium` clash with the component names in python (`velarium` is the component): use
  `set_audience()` / `set_velarium()` from scripts.
- Level_Amphitheatre had no lights; `Tools/Editor/setup_m10_view.py` added a directional light, sky atmosphere and a
  real-time sky light (Lumen on this GPU is software).
- Industry levels and soundwalk: see "Not done yet".

## How to verify by hand

1. Open `Level_Amphitheatre`, Play: the ray tracer runs (20 000 rays, R1–R4 + the cavea grid) and the heatmap colours
   the rows (C80 by default; metric selector in the panel). PlayerStart is above the cavea looking at the arena.
2. Select the `Amphitheatre` actor: tick **Audience** / **Velarium** (or in PIE: `Acoustics.Amphitheatre.Audience 1`,
   `Acoustics.Amphitheatre.Velarium 1`, then Run) and compare the heatmaps / R1–R4 metrics.
3. Headless experiments (editor closed):
   `UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <project> -run=pythonscript -script="<project>/Tools/Experiments/run_m10_experiments.py" -unattended -nopause -nosplash -NullRHI`
   → `Saved/Experiments/*.csv`, `m10_tables.md`.
