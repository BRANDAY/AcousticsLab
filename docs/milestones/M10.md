## Follow-up (16.10.2026): soundwalk, industry levels, experiments, fixes

### Soundwalk (menu 1.5, SPEC §0, §2.5.7, §3.7; S5)

- **Walker**: `Framework/AAcousticsWalkerPawn` (`ACharacter`, capsule 30 × 88 cm, camera / ears 1.6 m above the feet,
  `MaxStepHeight` 85 cm so one 0.75 m riser is one step, Space jumps, 3 m/s). `AAcousticsGameMode` spawns it instead of
  the flying explorer when the URL carries `?mode=Soundwalk` (`GetDefaultPawnClassForController`), on the tread of the
  R2 seat of `DA_AmphitheatrePositions` (row 10) facing the arena centre (`SpawnDefaultPawnFor`; the level PlayerStart
  floats above the cavea for the overview). The cavea / arena dynamic meshes have complex-as-simple collision (fast pass).
- **Live receiver**: `Audio/USoundwalkComponent` on the player controller (`?mode=Soundwalk` only). Every 0.1 s it takes
  the camera position, finds the nearest receiver of the last result (the 856 cavea cells and R1..R4) and, when the cell
  changes (hysteresis 0.3 m, at most one switch per 0.25 s), calls `UAuralizationComponent::SelectReceiver`. **Nothing is
  traced again** (SPEC §2.5.7): the source does not move, so the stored histograms of every cell stay valid.
- **On-demand impulse responses**: `SelectReceiver` now synthesizes the response of any receiver of the result from its
  histogram on first use (`EnsureImpulseResponse`, no WAV export), kept in a per-variant cache of
  `MaxOnDemandResponses` = 32 entries (oldest dropped first, never the assigned one). Switching the variant (2 / 3 / 4)
  keeps the cell. With the first result the walk switches from Dry to the solver's wet variant once (1 still gives Dry).
- **Ambient sources (S5)**: `Audio/ASoundwalkAmbientSource` — a looping 3D sound (engine panning + natural-sound falloff)
  at a fixed place: crowd murmur (row 12, opposite side), birds (above the top rows on the major axis), wind (top of the
  back wall). Clips are synthetic placeholders from `Tools/Audio/make_ambient_clips.py` (`/Game/Audio/Ambient/`). They
  play only in the soundwalk; **N** toggles them. They are *not* convolved with our responses (those belong to S1; each
  ambient source would need its own run) — documented simplification.
- **HUD / input**: the walk starts without the panel and with the mouse captured; **Tab** shows the panel with the
  cursor (and hides it again); the canvas HUD has a soundwalk key line and a green status line: listener position, row
  under the listener, the cell whose response is heard, its distance, C80 / T30 of that cell, number of switches,
  ambient state.
- **Ray count**: the ray tracer now bounds its per-chunk histogram memory by the receiver count
  (`FRayTracer::MaxChunksFor`, budget 512 MB, 16..128 chunks; still independent of the worker count), so the 860
  receivers no longer cap PIE at 40 000 rays: `Level_Amphitheatre` runs **100 000 rays** with the grid on BeginPlay
  (≈ 1 s here). The response of a 0.5 m cell 20–40 m from the source still rests on a few hundred ray hits — enough for
  C80 / EDT, the late tail of a single cell stays grainy (see E2).

### Experiments (headless; tables in `docs/results/m10b_tables.md`, CSVs in `docs/results/`)

**E2 — convergence over N (SPEC §5.2)**, 5 seeds per N, `RunRayConvergence`:
- Shoebox (α 0.1, s 0.2, closed room): T30 is within ±0.02 s of the 200k value already at 1 000 rays (sd 0.02 s) and
  within ±0.003 s from 20 000; C80 needs more: sd 0.4–0.5 dB at 1 000, 0.1–0.2 dB at 10–20 000, 0.03 dB at 200 000.
- Amphitheatre with audience (open, receivers 20–40 m away): below 20 000 rays most seeds see **no late energy at all**
  (C80 hits the 100 dB cap); C80 settles at 50 000–100 000 rays (sd 1–2 dB at R1 / R2, 2–4 dB at R3 / R4), T30 needs
  ≥ 100 000 at R1 (1.00 ± 0.05 s at 200 000) and stays undefined at R2 / R3 for most seeds even at 200 000.
  Answer to "how many rays": a closed room 10–20 k, an open amphitheatre ≥ 100–200 k per point receiver (the receiver
  sphere catches ~ N r² / 4d² rays).

**E4 — speed-up on the amphitheatre (SPEC §5.4)**, beam tracer through the M7 pool, 8160 triangles, R1..R4,

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 / 2 | 1.21 M | 38.5 s (min 27.8) | 9.8 s (min 8.3) | 3.9× (best 4.0× at 8) | 109.1 s | 2.8× |

The machine has 6 physical / 12 logical cores (M7); the order-2 tree is memory-bound (1.2 M nodes), so it stops scaling

**E5b — why the empty amphitheatre has no T30** (`RunEmptyDecayCheck`, 1 000 000 rays, R1..R4):

| case | R1 C80 / EDT | R2 | R3 | R4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fast pass: order ≤ 200, T_max 3 s, s 0.2 | −0.8 dB / 4.33 s | −1.9 / 5.29 | −1.9 / 4.16 | 3.7 / 4.28 |
| order ≤ 2000, T_max 3 s | identical | | | |
| order ≤ 2000, T_max 8 s | identical (EDT ±0.05 s) | | | |
| stone s 0.5 | 2.9 / 2.15 | 2.1 / 2.32 | 1.2 / 1.83 | 4.1 / 2.52 |
| stone s 0.8 | 5.8 / 1.31 | 4.5 / 1.59 | 1.9 / 1.78 | 5.8 / 1.41 |

The order cap and T_max change nothing, and 100 000 rays trace in 0.2 s: the energy is **not trapped** — most rays leave
over the top after a few reflections. A per-window breakdown (log of the 100 000-ray run) shows a sparse, echo-like
response: 20–110 ray hits per receiver, 37–47 % of the energy within 50 ms, 25–32 % at 200–500 ms in a handful of
strong late arrivals — specular reflections off the 20 concave risers that focus back toward the rows. The Schroeder
curve is a staircase, never a straight −35 dB decay, so T30 is undefined (correctly "n/a"); EDT of 4–5 s says the same.
More scattering of the stone breaks up the focusing (EDT 1.3–1.8 s at s 0.8). Real worn steps, seats and the podium
would scatter more than s 0.2; the preset stays at the SPEC value and the effect is documented.

- The hybrid on the amphitheatre needs a larger node budget than the default 500 000 (1.21 M at level 2 / order 2).

## Known simplifications (SPEC §6 and found here)

- Energy model only (no phase), broadband (NumBands = 1), no diffraction, no frequency-dependent scattering.
- Refracted beam with one apex (least-squares) — approximation (M6).
- Rows are offset ellipses with equal growth of both semi-axes, not true parallel curves of the arena ellipse (same rule
  as the positions asset); the rows are bare steps (no seats, no vomitoria, no podium wall, no outer façade).
- The audience is a material swap of the whole cavea shell (α 0.7) — no extra volume of people.
- The velarium is a flat absorbing ring at 18 m, not a sloped tensile roof.
- Open air: rays leaving over the top are lost (no sky / ground beyond the cavea), so late energy comes from the cavea
  and the arena only.
- Receivers are 0.5 m spheres; the per-cell metrics of the grid are noisy at 20 000 rays (few late hits per sphere at
  30–50 m); the per-row means smooth this.
- Geometry from `RenderData` needs Allow CPU Access (static meshes); the amphitheatre uses dynamic meshes.
- Industry plugins: IR only through the recorded click (M9), Audio Tracing not runnable on this GPU.

## Known issues

- **Empty amphitheatre: T30 undefined** — explained by E5b (sparse specular echoes off the concave risers, not trapped
  energy); EDT / C80 are the meaningful numbers there. More stone scattering removes most of it.
- **Steam Audio hears the empty cavea almost dry** (E6, C80 23–32 dB vs ≈ −1 dB of our tracers); not tuned.
- **Hybrid on the amphitheatre** with the default node budget (500 000) truncates the beam tree (1.21 M needed at level 2 /
  order 2) and then depends on the scheduling; raise `MaxBeamNodes` in the manager settings for hybrid runs there.
- Per-cell metrics of the grid are grainy at 100 000 rays (E2: C80 sd 1–4 dB per point receiver); the per-row means of
  E5 smooth this.
- The soundwalk ambient sources are not auralized with our responses (engine 3D panning only).
- `LogSynthesis: Invalid impulse sample rate: 0` at every level start (the convolution preset before the first IR): benign.
- Industry levels: no audience / velarium toggles (baked empty cavea); Audio Tracing not runnable on this GPU.
- The fast-pass issues (HUD description, velarium over the heatmap, PIE ray limit, python name clash, lights) are fixed
  (see "Fixes of the fast pass").

## How to verify by hand

1. Open `Level_Amphitheatre`, Play: the ray tracer runs (20 000 rays, R1–R4 + the cavea grid) and the heatmap colours
   the rows (C80 by default; metric selector in the panel). PlayerStart is above the cavea looking at the arena.
2. Select the `Amphitheatre` actor: tick **Audience** / **Velarium** (or in PIE: `Acoustics.Amphitheatre.Audience 1`,
   `Acoustics.Amphitheatre.Velarium 1`, then Run) and compare the heatmaps / R1–R4 metrics.
3. **Soundwalk**: main menu → 1.5. You stand on row 10 facing the arena; the clip plays convolved with the response of
   the nearest cavea cell (green HUD line: cell, distance, C80 / T30). Walk up and down the rows (WASD, Space jumps):
   the cell name changes and the sound with it; 1 = Dry, 2 = Ray; N = ambient sources; Tab = panel and cursor.
4. **Steam Audio level**: menu 2.1 — the baked cavea with Steam Audio Geometry; 5 / 2 switch Steam Audio / our ray
   response (M9 keys); `Acoustics.Industry.MeasureClick` measures the plugin's response at R1..R4.
5. Headless experiments (editor closed):
   `UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <project> -run=pythonscript -script="<project>/Tools/Experiments/run_m10_experiments.py" -unattended -nopause -nosplash -NullRHI`
   → `Saved/Experiments/*.csv`, `m10_tables.md`; the follow-up: `run_m10b_experiments.py e5b e2 e3` (same command) →
   `m10b_tables.md`, and `Tools/Benchmark/run_m7_benchmark.py amphitheatre` for E4.
